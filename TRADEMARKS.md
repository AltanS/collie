It does not give you any right to the ColliePWA name or its logo. This page says how you may use
them.

## The marks

- The name **ColliePWA**, and its short form **Collie**, used as the name of this software or of a
  service built on it.
- The **ColliePWA logo**: the dog mark, in every form it ships in. That includes the favicons and
  app icons under `web/public/`, the social card in `assets/`, and the ColliePWA wordmark.
- The domain **colliepwa.dev**.

## What you may do without asking

- Say what is true: "a fork of ColliePWA", "based on ColliePWA", "works with ColliePWA".
- Write about ColliePWA, teach it, review it, and link to it, by name.
- Run an official release or a build from this repository, unchanged, and call it ColliePWA.

## What needs a new name

**A fork must have its own name.** If you change the code and publish or distribute the result,
give it a name that is not ColliePWA, not Collie, and not easy to confuse with either. The same
applies to a hosted service built on this code. You may still say it is a fork of ColliePWA, as
above.

**A fork must not use the logo.** Remove the dog mark and the wordmark from your build, your icons,
your site and your store listing. Draw your own. The MIT License lets you keep the code, but the
logo is not part of that grant.

## What needs written permission

- An app in the Apple App Store, Google Play, or any other app store under the ColliePWA name, or
  with the logo.
- A package or installer published under the ColliePWA name. The official packages are maintained
  in-house.
- The logo on merchandise, or anywhere it could suggest that we endorse you.

To ask, open an issue on this repository.

## App stores

ColliePWA is not in the App Store or Google Play. You add it to your home screen from the browser,
and that is the only way to install it on a phone.
