  11. Kumquat: It tastes sour on the outside and sweet inside. You eat the whole fruit, skin included.
  12. Peach: It tastes sweet and juicy. The skin is fuzzy. Ripe peaches have a soft, floral aroma.
  13. Nectarine: It tastes sweet and sharp. The skin is smooth with no fuzz. The flavor is like a peach.
