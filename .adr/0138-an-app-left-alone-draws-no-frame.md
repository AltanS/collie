# 0138: An app left alone draws no frame

- **Status:** Superseded by [ADR 0148](./0148-a-theme-names-words-never-drawings-and-the-device-choice-wins.md) (2026-11-16)
- **Decided:** A page left alone asks for no frame, after issue 748 (an always-moving mark drove a phone's browser out of file handles). Its first rules, no animation at rest and no motion setting, were reversed by its own amendments: the mark now drifts in steps while the page is visible, and a "Character moves" switch stops it (see ADR 0148).
